个人简介About Me

我是南方科技大学计算机科学与工程系的博士研究生。主要研究方向包括法律判决预测 (Legal Judgment Prediction)、知识图谱补全及推理 (Knowledge Graph Completion and Reasoning)、以及大语言模型 (Large Language Models, LLMs)。致力于结合预训练语言模型与图神经推理机制，解决复杂法律场景下的可解释性与语义证据回溯问题。
博士团队：主导师：甘晓华院士；第二导师：杨双华院士。I am a researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). My research interests include Legal AI, Legal Judgment Prediction (LJP), and Knowledge Graph Completion & Reasoning. I focus on combining pre-trained language models with graph neural reasoning to improve interpretability and clue tracing in judicial scenarios.
Doctoral supervision: Primary supervisor: Prof. Xiaohua Gan, CAE Academician; co-supervisor: Prof. Shuang-Hua Yang, CAE Academician.
