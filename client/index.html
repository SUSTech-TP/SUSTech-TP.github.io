个人简介About Me

我是南方科技大学计算机科学与工程系的研究人员。主要研究方向包括法律人工智能 (Legal AI)、法律判决预测 (Legal Judgment Prediction) 与 知识图谱补全及推理 (Knowledge Graph Completion)。致力于结合预训练语言模型与图神经推理机制，解决复杂法律场景下的可解释性与语义证据回溯问题。I am a researcher in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech). My research interests include Legal AI, Legal Judgment Prediction (LJP), and Knowledge Graph Completion & Reasoning. I focus on combining pre-trained language models with graph neural reasoning to improve interpretability and clue tracing in judicial scenarios.
